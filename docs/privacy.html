Fretboard Freedom for iPad

Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

Fretboard Freedom doesn't collect any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers, and the app doesn't send anything over the internet.

The microphone

The app asks for the microphone so it can hear your guitar and check each note you play. The sound is analyzed on your iPad, a few milliseconds at a time, while you practice. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. You can turn the microphone off for the app at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone; you can still tap notes on the neck to play them.

Your progress and settings

Your grades, tempos and practice settings are saved on your iPad, inside the app's own storage. They are included in your iPad's backups (iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer) under Apple's terms, the same as any app's data. Nobody else, including the developer, can see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

The practice coach

On iPads with Apple Intelligence, a coach writes a tip after each pass. It uses Apple's on-device language model: your grades are processed on your iPad and aren't sent to the developer or to anyone else. Without Apple Intelligence, the app shows its own built-in tips instead.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
