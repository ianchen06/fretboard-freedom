Fretboard Freedom for iPad

Support

You play your guitar; the iPad listens and checks every note. Find any note on the neck, practice scales and arpeggios, trade licks with a backing band, tighten your rhythm, and follow a daily session that puts it all together.

What you need

  • An iPad with iPadOS 17 or later.
  • A guitar. An acoustic, or an electric through an amp, near the iPad works with the built-in mic.

Common questions

The app doesn't hear my guitar

Check that the mic is allowed in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone › Fretboard Freedom. Then watch the level meter while you play: if it barely moves, play louder or move the iPad closer to the guitar or amp.

Can I plug in instead of using the mic?

Yes. Connect a USB audio interface, or an amp with USB audio, to the iPad with a USB-C cable that carries data (a charge-only cable won't work). Pick it in the Hearing panel. A cable hears only the guitar, so the click can't leak in. A clean sound with few effects gives the surest notes.

My notes are graded late or early

Bluetooth headphones and speakers can add a delay to the click. For the most accurate timing, use wired headphones. Headphones also keep the mic from hearing the click.

How do I start over?

Your progress is stored only on your iPad. Deleting the app and installing it again starts fresh.
