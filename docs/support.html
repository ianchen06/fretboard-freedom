Fretboard Freedom for iPad

Support

You play your guitar; the iPad listens and checks every note. Practice the five pentatonic boxes in any key, up and down, in 3s, in 4s and in 3rds, against a click that speeds up as you play cleanly.

What you need

  • An iPad with iPadOS 26 or later.
  • A guitar. An acoustic, or an electric through an amp, near the iPad works with the built-in mic.
  • For the practice coach's written tips: an iPad that supports Apple Intelligence, with it turned on. Without it, the app shows its own tips.

Common questions

The app doesn't hear my guitar

Check that the mic is allowed in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone › Fretboard Freedom. Then watch the level meter while you play: if it barely moves, play louder or move the iPad closer to the guitar or amp.

Can I plug in instead of using the mic?

Yes. Connect a USB audio interface, or an amp with USB audio, to the iPad with a USB-C cable that carries data (a charge-only cable won't work). The app switches to it, and the input button in the Hearing panel shows its name. Tap that button to switch back to the iPad's mic or to pick the channel your guitar is on. A cable hears only the guitar, so the click can't leak in. A clean sound with few effects gives the surest notes.

My notes are graded late or early

Bluetooth headphones and speakers can add a delay to the click. For the most accurate timing, use wired headphones. Headphones also keep the mic from hearing the click.

The coach only shows short tips

The longer tips come from Apple Intelligence on your iPad. If your iPad doesn't support it, or it's turned off or still downloading, the app uses its built-in tips, and the card says why.

Can I practice without a guitar nearby?

Yes. Tap the notes on the neck to hear them. Taps count as played, though not for timing.

How do I start over?

Your progress is stored only on your iPad. Deleting the app and installing it again starts fresh.
