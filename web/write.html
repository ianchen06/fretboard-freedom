      How to use this page
      1. 1
        Chords first. Pick a style and a progression for each part. Play it and hum over it.
      2. 2
        Write a line, get a tune. ✨ sets the words to the chords so the melody moves the way the tones do. Or ● record yourself playing or humming it.
      3. 3
        Fix the red. A red link means the tune moves against the tones, so a word may be misheard (倒字). Move a note or change the word.
