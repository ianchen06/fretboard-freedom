    How it’s built
    1. 1
      Blocks. Warm-up, technique, fretboard, scales, arpeggios, connecting the neck, improvising, review. A 2-minute break about every 30 minutes.
    2. 2
      Rules pick each exercise. The next shape on your path is always in. The rest are what you’re ready for but haven’t nailed, what’s due for review, and never the same thing twice in a row.
    3. 3
      Theory builds the notes. Every shape is computed from the scale, in any key, so no dot is wrong.
    4. 4
      Your playing steers it. The mic grades each exercise. That moves your skill map, the tempo ladder and when each shape comes back.
