How to answer
  1. 1
    Listen first. Hum the lick back in your head while the band plays it.
  2. 2
    Find the landing note. Every lick ends on a chord tone. Aim for it, then fill in the way there.
  3. 3
    Copy the feel. Bends, slides and vibrato matter as much as the notes. Play it like it’s yours.
